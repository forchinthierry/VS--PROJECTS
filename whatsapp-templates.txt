WHATSAPP MESSAGE TEMPLATES — G-SPIRIT LOAN SOLUTION
=====================================================
Submit these in Meta's WhatsApp Manager under "Message Templates."
Category: UTILITY (these are status updates about an existing request,
not marketing — utility templates are cheaper and approved faster).
Language: English

{{1}}, {{2}} etc. are variables Meta will ask you to define — they get
filled in automatically by the Worker code when a message is sent.
